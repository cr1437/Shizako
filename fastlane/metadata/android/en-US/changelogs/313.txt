First F-Droid release (zako3.13, versionCode 313).

• Dedicated F-Droid build variant: in-app updater and update checks are disabled, one-click third-party APK installation is hidden, and the AI explain feature (proprietary providers) is removed.
• The bundled compatibility stub APK is now built from source during the build instead of being committed as a binary.
• Chinese Maven mirrors are automatically disabled for this build variant, so only official repositories are used.
